How to Become Muslim

Accepting Islam is simple. Your belief is a personal relationship between you and God, so you do not need anyone else present to declare your faith. By making this declaration, you affirm that there is only one true God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, including all the prophets who came before him. Follow the steps below.

1

Step One

“I testify that there is no one worthy of worship except Allah, and I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.”
2

Step Two

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللهِ

“Ash-hadu an la ilaha illa-Allah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan Rasul-Allah”

3

Step Three

While this is optional, it is definitely encouraged. We recommend finding a mosque near you to connect with local Muslims who can guide you in the right direction. It is also a beautiful opportunity to formally declare your Shahada, though taking it alone is valid and sufficient.
